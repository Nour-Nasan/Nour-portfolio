FEATURED PROJECT

Syrian System for Marketing & Handicrafts

A full-stack marketplace platform designed to connect buyers with artisans and small businesses. The system includes shop and product management, basket and orders, order tracking, direct messaging, advertisements, flash offers, bazaars, notifications and AI-assisted gift recommendations.

Live Demo GitHub
Python Django MySQL Bootstrap JavaScript Groq AI

KEY FEATURES

What the system includes

Shops & Products

Sellers can manage shops, categories and products, while buyers can browse and filter available items.

Basket & Orders

Buyers can add products to a basket, place orders and choose delivery and contact methods.

Order Tracking

Orders move through preparation, shipping and delivery stages with visible tracking updates.

Direct Messaging

Buyers and sellers can communicate directly through conversations inside the platform.

AI Gift Recommendation

The system suggests suitable gifts based on budget, occasion and recipient details.

Advertisements & Offers

Sellers can publish advertisements and create time-limited flash offers for their products.

Bazaars

Sellers can browse bazaars and request table bookings managed by bazaar organizers.

Notifications

Users receive updates related to orders, comments, bookings and other important events.
